# Marketing landing page

- **Scope and mode:** Public `/` route for Persuade mode; the operational product remains at `/app`.
- **Audience:** School owners, board members, principals, finance and operations leaders evaluating a replacement or consolidation platform.
- **Visitor job:** Understand the platform quickly, trust its governance and international fit, compare a recommended rollout package and open the product preview.
- **Primary action:** Open the product preview. Secondary actions are exploring capabilities and reviewing pricing.
- **Proof and content:** A school-day operating ledger; canonical people and household records; traceable balances and immutable journals; offline-safe attendance; restricted student-support access; modular country and integration contracts; validated migration stages.
- **Direction:** The school-day operating ledger extends the approved Operational Ledger identity into a persuasive, spacious public surface. A vertical sweep joins admissions, attendance, finance, care and transport in the first viewport.
- **Memorable moment:** One illustrative campus-day timeline visibly turns disconnected departments into a single governed record.
- **Constraints:** Do not fabricate customers, benchmarks, certifications or production data. Label illustrative data. Present BDT prices as recommended launch pricing, not validated willingness-to-pay. Preserve WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion, responsive behavior and the existing product shell.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Final public product name, logo and brand assets; production sales/contact destination; customer proof; final pricing after school interviews and willingness-to-pay research.
